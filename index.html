Curatorial premise

Context

At a technological threshold.

The series belongs to a narrow moment in generative-image history: Midjourney V1 was in its first documented phase; OpenAI's DALL·E 2 red-team preview period had not yet begun; Stable Diffusion would not be publicly released until August 2022.

18 FEB

19 FEB

Putin #002 through #017 recorded.

22 FEB

Putin #018 through #040 recorded; final event at 12:53:37 UTC.

On 24 February 2022, Russian armed forces began the full-scale invasion of Ukraine. The project makes no predictive or causal claim about this chronology; the date is presented as documented historical context.
