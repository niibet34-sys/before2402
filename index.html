40 Machine Portraits of Vladimir Putin

A complete forty-work sequence recorded on OpenSea over five days in February 2022 — presented as an archive of early machine portraiture, digital provenance and historical context.

Curatorial premise

The archive is not presented as prophecy. It is presented as evidence — of a machine trying to stabilize a face, and of a date that later changed how the images are encountered.

Context

At a technological threshold.

The series belongs to a narrow moment in generative-image history: Midjourney V1 was in its first documented phase; OpenAI's DALL·E 2 red-team preview period had not yet begun; Stable Diffusion would not be publicly released until August 2022.
